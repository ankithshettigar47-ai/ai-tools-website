# 🚀 CEO Social Media Command Center

A fully automated social media management platform designed for CEOs who want complete control with minimal effort. All departments work autonomously - you just approve and upload!

## 📋 Features

### 🔍 Research Department
- **Trend Scanning**: Automatically discovers trending topics in your niche
- **Competitor Analysis**: Tracks competitor performance and strategies
- **Smart Recommendations**: Suggests content ideas based on trending data

### 🎬 Content Creation Department
- **Auto Video Generation**: Creates complete video scripts with hooks, content, and CTAs
- **Story Creation**: Generates engaging multi-slide story content
- **Full Campaign Mode**: Creates entire content campaigns (3 videos + 2 stories) with one click
- **Smart Hashtags**: Auto-generates relevant hashtags for maximum reach

### ✅ Approval Queue
- **CEO Review Dashboard**: Preview all content before publishing
- **One-Click Approval**: Approve and upload to Instagram instantly
- **Request Changes**: Send content back for revisions with feedback
- **Reject Option**: Remove unwanted content from queue

### 📊 Analytics Department
- **Performance Tracking**: Monitor posts, likes, followers, and engagement
- **Visual Charts**: See your growth with beautiful performance graphs
- **Smart Insights**: Get actionable recommendations based on data

### ⚙️ Settings
- **Instagram Integration**: Connect your Instagram account
- **Content Categories**: Choose your niches (Tech, Lifestyle, Business, etc.)
- **Auto-Upload Threshold**: Set preferences for automatic posting

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

- **HTML5** - Semantic structure
- **CSS3** - Modern gradient designs with animations
- **Vanilla JavaScript** - No frameworks, pure performance
- **LocalStorage** - Persistent data storage

## 🚀 Quick Start

1. Open `index.html` in any modern web browser
2. Navigate through the tabs:
   - **Research**: Scan trends and analyze competitors
   - **Content**: Generate videos, stories, or full campaigns
   - **Approval**: Review and approve content for upload
   - **Analytics**: Track your performance
   - **Settings**: Configure your account

## 💡 Keyboard Shortcuts

- `Ctrl/Cmd + 1` - Research Tab
- `Ctrl/Cmd + 2` - Content Tab
- `Ctrl/Cmd + 3` - Approval Tab
- `Ctrl/Cmd + 4` - Analytics Tab
- `Ctrl/Cmd + 5` - Settings Tab
- `Escape` - Close modal

## 🎯 How It Works

1. **Research Phase**: Click "Scan Latest Trends" to discover viral topics
2. **Creation Phase**: 
   - Auto-create from trends, OR
   - Generate standalone videos/stories, OR
   - Create full campaigns
3. **Approval Phase**: Review content in the Approval Queue
4. **Upload Phase**: Click "Approve & Upload to Instagram" to publish
